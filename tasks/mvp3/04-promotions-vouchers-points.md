# 04 — Promotions, Vouchers & Points Assessment

**Status:** WIP / Requires product and accounting approval  
**Outcome:** Decide whether any legacy reward mechanic belongs in the Philippine revamp without compromising financial transparency.

## Scope

- Assess referral points, login/investment rewards, voucher store, promo codes, red packets, fee discounts, and bonus-interest coupons.
- Identify funding source, accounting/tax treatment, eligibility, limits, expiration, reversal, abuse controls, and investor disclosure.
- Separate marketing rewards from loan principal, contractual investor return, and core ledger calculations.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each retained reward has an owner, business case, cost/funding account, tax treatment, and measurable success criterion.
- Rewards cannot modify historical loan terms or obscure gross return, fee, tax, and net return.
- Unapproved legacy voucher types are explicitly retired and excluded from migration.

## Open decisions

- Whether to retain any reward program; this feature is not part of the supplied target overview.
