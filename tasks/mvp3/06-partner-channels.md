# 06 — Partner Channel Assessment

**Status:** WIP / Requires commercial and security approval  
**Outcome:** Decide whether any legacy partner-specific registration, investment, wallet, or withdrawal channel should be rebuilt.

## Scope

- Assess PitakaMo, NUWallet, legacy regional aggregation, white-label access, and partner authentication/settlement behavior.
- Confirm contract status, active users/volume, data ownership, support responsibility, compliance, settlement, and exit obligations.
- Define a standard partner API/security model if a current business case exists.

## Acceptance criteria

- No partner integration is rebuilt without a named commercial owner and current signed requirement.
- Partner actions use scoped credentials, user consent/linkage, idempotency, audit, limits, and settlement reconciliation.
- Static shared secrets, target-user impersonation, and partner-specific bypasses are not carried forward.

## Open decisions

- Active partner commitments, migration/termination obligations, supported actions, and settlement model.
