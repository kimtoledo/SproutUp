# 03 — Advanced Risk & Portfolio Analytics

**Status:** WIP / Requires confirmation  
**Outcome:** Credit and management teams can identify concentration, deterioration, and portfolio trends beyond baseline operations reporting.

## Scope

- Exposure and performance by borrower, industry, risk grade, cohort, term, product, and investor concentration.
- Expected-versus-actual cash flow, delinquency roll rates, vintage analysis, and scenario views.
- Model/version monitoring and controlled exports for governance review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every metric has an approved definition, data lineage, cutoff, and owner.
- Analytics reconcile to loan, investment, collection, and ledger control totals.
- Model outputs are advisory unless a separately approved workflow authorizes automated decisions.
- Historical results retain the metric/model definition used when produced.

## Open decisions

- Priority risk questions, model governance, forecasting method, data history, and acceptable use of predictive models.
