# 05 — Content & App Communication Assessment

**Status:** WIP / Requires product confirmation  
**Outcome:** Decide the minimum owned content-management surface versus external tools.

## Scope

- Assess legacy newsflash, banners, videos, media library, homepage campaign content, app-version notices, email blasts, and push notifications.
- Separate mandatory transactional communications from marketing content.
- Evaluate a small internal CMS versus managed marketing/content platforms.

## Acceptance criteria

- Retained content types have approval, scheduling, audience, expiry, accessibility, audit, and security rules.
- Marketing messages include required consent, unsubscribe/suppression, and delivery controls.
- App-version enforcement cannot block users without an approved support and rollback path.

## Open decisions

- Content owners, channels, localization, approval workflow, and build-versus-buy direction.
