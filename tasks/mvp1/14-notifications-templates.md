# 14 — Notifications & Templates

**Status:** WIP  
**Outcome:** Users and staff receive reliable notices for critical lifecycle events.

## Scope

- Queued email and SMS delivery with retries, provider response, failure status, and admin visibility.
- Versioned templates for registration, OTP, KYC, credit, campaign, commitment, disbursement, repayment, distribution, and withdrawal events.
- In-app notification feed for material asynchronous events.
- Recipient preferences only where notices are legally/operationally optional.

## Acceptance criteria

- Security and financial notifications cannot be disabled when mandatory.
- Duplicate job execution does not send duplicate transaction notices.
- Templates render safely with required variables and no sensitive data leakage.
- Support can trace an event to delivery attempts without viewing secrets.
- Failed critical notifications are visible for operational follow-up.

## Dependencies

- Tasks 02–12 — registration, KYC, credit, campaign, commitment, wallet, disbursement, repayment, and distribution events notified on are generated by these stages.
