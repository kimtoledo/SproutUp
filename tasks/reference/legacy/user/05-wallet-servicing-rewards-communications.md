# 05 — Wallet, Servicing, Rewards & Communications

## Wallet and servicing

- Top-up: bank-transfer evidence plus Paynamics, Coins.ph, NuWallet, PayPal, and PitakaMo-related surfaces in the wider legacy code.
- Withdrawal: bank selection, request submission, optional OTP, admin review, and transaction/activity visibility.
- Statements: investment records, borrower repayments, wallet transactions, contracts, and attachments are exposed through several different views.
- Notifications: in-app list/detail, email templates/queues, SMS/OTP, push notifications, and public announcements exist across applications.

## Growth and loyalty features

- Auto-invest rules with agreement, enable/disable, edit, and delete.
- Referral invite/edit/reinvite/delete/activity plus a legacy multi-level progress experience.
- Points history/login rewards.
- Voucher availability, purchase/buyout, redemption, history, and investment linking.
- Elite-circle and reserve-member experiences.

## Target disposition

- MVP 1: ledger-backed manual deposits and withdrawals, transaction history, statements/receipts, essential email/SMS/in-app notices, and support contact paths.
- MVP 2: one payment provider, auto-invest, approved one-level referrals, and richer self-service/communications.
- MVP 3 or retire: points, vouchers, elite/reserve programs, broad marketing content, and provider-specific legacy flows.

The target referral model is one level only and funded from platform commission. Do not reproduce legacy levels or finance rewards from investor principal/returns.
