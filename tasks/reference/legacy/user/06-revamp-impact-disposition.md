# 06 — Revamp Impact & Disposition

## MVP 1 — rebuild as coherent journeys

- Public product explanation, eligibility, disclosures, and support.
- Secure registration, login, recovery, MFA/OTP, and session management.
- Separate but consistent SME borrower and investor onboarding.
- Document upload, consent/signature, status tracking, correction, and approval notices.
- Investor marketplace, campaign detail, commitment, portfolio, and distributions.
- Borrower application, offer acceptance, funding status, loan schedule, and repayment history.
- Manual-bank wallet funding/withdrawal, transaction history, receipts, and notifications.

## MVP 2

- Auto-invest, one-level referrals, payment-gateway automation, notification preferences, and expanded self-service.

## MVP 3 or retire

- Multi-level referral progress, points, vouchers, elite circle, reserve listings, legacy provider screens, and external-accounting imports unless approved.

## UX migration rule

Do not port the hash routes and screen hierarchy literally. Preserve validated user outcomes, data, consent evidence, contracts, balances, and active obligations. Design the new journeys around explicit states and next actions, with mobile accessibility and plain Philippine terminology.
