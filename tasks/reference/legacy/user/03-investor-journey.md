# 03 — Investor Journey

## Journey observed

1. Complete investor profile, KYC, bank details, suitability/CKA, acknowledgements, and risk acceptance.
2. Await admin approval and contract/signature requirements.
3. Top up funds through bank transfer or provider-specific paths.
4. Browse published investments; reserve-group members may see restricted listings.
5. View listing details, attachments, calculator output, and contract information.
6. Submit an investment/pledge; optionally link a voucher or use auto-invest.
7. View portfolio investments, repayment plan/records, activities, notifications, and transactions.
8. Withdraw available funds, subject to bank details, OTP, balance, and admin/provider processing.

## API-backed capabilities observed

- Loan filter, view, calculator, interest/pledge, invest, and on-hold confirmation.
- Investment list, cancel, and voucher linking.
- Auto-invest list/view/update/agreement/enable/delete.
- Wallet top-up and withdrawal requests.
- Profile, bank, OTP, settings, activity, notification, transaction, and contract access.

## Revamp MVP 1 direction

Retain approved onboarding, a transparent campaign marketplace, manual-bank cash in/out, commitments, portfolio/repayment visibility, statements, and notifications. Defer auto-invest, vouchers/points, provider channels, and restricted reserve programs unless separately approved.

## Acceptance risks to resolve

- Available-balance and reservation timing during concurrent commitments.
- Oversubscription and partial allocation rules.
- Campaign cancellation/refund behavior.
- Gross return, tax, fee, and net distribution disclosure.
- Contract version bound to the exact commitment.
- Investor classification renewal and restriction handling.
