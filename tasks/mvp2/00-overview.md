# MVP 2 — Automation & Growth Overview

**Status:** WIP  
**Goal:** Scale the approved pilot model without weakening ledger, compliance, or maker-checker controls.

## Included

- Automated cash-in, webhook processing, and payout integration
- Investor auto-invest rules and controlled allocation
- One-level lifetime referral attribution and commission ledger
- Collections reminders, delinquency workflows, and servicing automation
- Role-based dashboards and portfolio/business intelligence
- Accounting/tax journals, schedules, and certificate automation
- Borrower/investor self-service improvements
- Capacity, resilience, security, and operational hardening

## Not included

- Multi-level referral hierarchies or override commissions
- Rewards funded from investor principal or investor returns
- External bookkeeping ingestion such as Xero/QuickBooks
- Secondary-market investment trading
- Multi-currency or multi-country operations unless separately approved

## Release gates

- Automated money movement reconciles to provider and internal ledger records.
- Referral calculations use only recognized platform commission under approved eligibility rules.
- Automation has idempotency, retry, exception, and manual-recovery paths.
- Capacity and security testing supports the approved growth target.
