# 05 — Dashboards & Business Intelligence

**Status:** WIP  
**Outcome:** Management and operating teams can monitor funnel, portfolio, revenue, risk, and service performance.

## Scope

- Role-specific dashboards for origination funnel, campaigns, investments, portfolio, collections, cash, fees, tax, and referrals.
- Agreed metric definitions, filters, drill-downs, scheduled exports, and reporting snapshots.
- Data freshness, lineage, reconciliation, and access controls.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each metric has an owner, formula, source, cutoff, timezone, and refresh expectation.
- Financial dashboard totals reconcile to approved ledger/report totals.
- Users can trace aggregate figures to authorized underlying records.
- Historical snapshots do not silently change when definitions are revised.

## Open decisions

- KPI catalogue, analytics architecture, retention, refresh frequency, and executive dashboard priorities.
