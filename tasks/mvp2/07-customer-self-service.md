# 07 — Customer Self-Service

**Status:** WIP  
**Outcome:** Borrowers and investors can resolve common account and transaction needs without staff intervention.

## Scope

- Profile/document updates with re-verification triggers.
- Statements, payment schedules, receipts, tax documents, contracts, and downloadable transaction history.
- Borrower payment evidence and case status; investor withdrawal and bank-verification status.
- Notification preferences for optional communications and support-request intake.

## Acceptance criteria

- Users can access only their own authorized documents and transactions.
- Sensitive changes trigger step-up authentication and required review.
- Generated documents identify the data cutoff and remain reproducible.
- Self-service actions create auditable workflow events and do not bypass controls.

## Open decisions

- Supported service requests, turnaround targets, document formats, and secure-delivery duration.
