# 08 — Scale, Security & Operations Hardening

**Status:** WIP  
**Outcome:** The platform can grow beyond the pilot with tested capacity, resilience, and incident controls.

## Scope

- Performance/load testing for campaign opening, investment bursts, batch distributions, and reports.
- Security review, dependency scanning, penetration testing, access review, and key rotation.
- Queue/job observability, dead-letter recovery, disaster recovery, incident runbooks, and service objectives.
- Data lifecycle, archival, privacy request, and operational capacity procedures.

## Acceptance criteria

- Approved load targets pass without overfunding, duplicate ledger posting, or unacceptable latency.
- Critical security findings are resolved before wider release.
- Recovery exercises meet approved recovery objectives and preserve ledger integrity.
- On-call staff can detect, stop, reconcile, and recover failed financial jobs using documented procedures.

## Open decisions

- Growth forecast, service objectives, recovery targets, audit cadence, and compliance certification roadmap.
